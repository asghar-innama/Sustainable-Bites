The Sustainable Bites food waste management system is a web-based platform that connects donors and recipients of excess/leftover food. Donors can register on the website and submit donation forms, including information about the type of food, quantity, and location. Recipients, such as NGOs and soup kitchens, can also register on the website and raise requests for excess/leftover food. The system matches donors and recipients based on their needs and takes into account factors such as distance and food type. Once a match is made, the recipient can contact the donor to arrange for pick-up of the excess/leftover food
